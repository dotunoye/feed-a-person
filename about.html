THE MOMENT THAT MOVED US

Matriculation Day.
A reason to act.

It started on Matriculation Day at Covenant University when we saw a fellow student begging for food. We realized the solution to local hunger was already sitting there in the form of surplus event catering. FAP rapidly mobilizes to intercept unserved food from ceremonies and redistributes it to orphanages and vulnerable households.
