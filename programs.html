OUR WORK

Good food.
On its way to good.

Food retrieval and redistribution. For orphanages and low-income households.

01 / UNIVERSITY FOOD DRIVES

From campus events.
To fuller plates.

Food retrieval and redistribution starts with surplus catering. We coordinate collection from university ceremonies and food-centered businesses, then repackage and redistribute good leftover food.

Connect. Collect. Redistribute.

Be part of this
02 / ORPHANAGE OUTREACH

More nourishment.
More room to grow.

Orphanages are a core target of our food redistribution. We coordinate with children’s homes to bring retrieved food where it is needed.

Care that reaches beyond campus.

Be part of this
03 / LOW-INCOME HOUSEHOLD RELIEF

One less worry.
One shared meal.

Low-income households are a core target of our food redistribution. We connect good leftover food with families who need it.

Practical support. Personal dignity.
